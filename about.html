Crafted for Cyclists, by Cyclists

15+ years of performance cycling passion, master mechanic workshop heritage, precision bike fitting, and community camaraderie.

Established 2011

From a Small Repair Bench to California's Premier Cycling Hub

VeloPeak began with a single repair stand and a simple mission: build bicycles properly, tune them to exact tolerances, and treat every rider — from weekend commuters to category racers — with genuine mechanical passion.

Over the last fifteen years, we have expanded into a 4,000-square-foot flagship showroom, dynamic laser fitting studio, and full-service master workshop, while retaining our core obsession with precision assembly and personal service.

Our Guiding Principles

The VeloPeak Cycling Philosophy

We believe a properly fitted bicycle transforms daily life and opens new horizons.

Human-Centered Fit

A bike is only as good as how comfortable you feel on hour four of a ride. We tune touchpoints before you roll out.

Zero-Compromise Build

Every bearing greased, every spoke measured with a dial gauge, and every cable trimmed to optimal aerodynamic arc.

Inclusive Community

From first-time kid riders to veteran endurance cyclists, everyone gets the same expert guidance without intimidation.

Master Mechanics

Workshop Heritage & Tooling

Our mechanics hold professional certifications from the Barnett Bicycle Institute, Shimano T.E.C., SRAM Technical University, and Park Tool. We invest in industrial ultrasonic cleaners, digital torque wrenches, and spoke tensiometers because precision matters.

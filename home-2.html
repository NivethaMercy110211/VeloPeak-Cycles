Pre-Ride Routine

The Essential ABC Pre-Ride Check

Taking 60 seconds before heading out extends component life and ensures safety on fast descents.

A is for Air

Check PSI ratings on your tire sidewall. Road tires require 70-90 PSI; mountain tubeless 22-28 PSI.

B is for Brakes

Squeeze both levers. Ensure at least one finger width between lever and bar at full bite point.

C is for Chain & Cassette

Spin cranks backward. Inspect for dry links, dirt buildup, and apply wet/dry ceramic lube.

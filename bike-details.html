Built for Speed, Distance & Compliance

The Apex R1 is Altura Cycleworks' benchmark endurance performance platform. Designed for cyclists who demand the climbing stiffness of an all-out race machine without the bone-jarring fatigue on rough country asphalt.

Featuring a dropped seatstay configuration, flattened chainstays for vertical compliance, and an oversized BB86 bottom bracket shell that converts every watt of pedal torque into forward momentum.

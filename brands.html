We partner exclusively with frame builders, component innovators, and technical gear manufacturers who share our passion for precision craftsmanship and lifetime durability.

Performance Road Flagship

Altura Cycleworks

Altura is dedicated to the relentless pursuit of road aerodynamics and lightweight carbon matrix technology. Founded by former aerospace engineers, each monocoque frame is molded with directional Toray fibers to balance instantaneous power transfer with smooth road dampening.

Sub-850g raw frame weights
Computational Fluid Dynamic wind-tunnel tube profiles
Manufacturer of the award-winning Apex R1 and AeroStorm Pro
Explore Altura Bicycles
Singletrack & Enduro

RidgeForm Rigs

Born in the rugged mountain singletracks of the Pacific Northwest, RidgeForm builds aggressive trail, all-mountain, and enduro bikes designed to soak up brutal rock gardens and big drop landings.

Proprietary 4-bar linkage suspension kinematics
Overbuilt sealed Enduro MAX cartridge pivot bearings
Home of the Summit X7 and Enduro 9 rigs
Explore RidgeForm Rigs
Urban & All-Road

Vektor Bikes

Vektor bridges the gap between daily urban commuting speed and weekend gravel exploration. Utilizing double-butted 6061 alloy with stealth internal cable routing and generous tire clearance.

Integrated pannier, mudguard and bottle bosses
Ergonomic cockpits with puncture-resistant contact patch
Builders of the MetroFlow Hybrid and RidgeForm GX
Explore Vektor Builds
E-Mobility Pioneers

Veltric E-Mobility

Veltric is redefining modern city transportation. Their mid-drive motors feature natural torque-sensing algorithms that seamlessly amplify your pedal effort without jerky acceleration or noise.

Integrated 500Wh LG lithium-ion battery cells
90km real-world pedal assist range
Builders of the VoltRide E2 commuter series

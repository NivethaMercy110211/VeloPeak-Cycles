Visiting VeloPeak Cycles

Helpful guidelines for test riding bikes and workshop drop-offs.

Please bring a valid photo ID, a credit card, and your helmet (or borrow one of our sanitized test helmets). If you ride clip-in pedals (SPD or Look), feel free to bring your cycling shoes and our mechanics will install your pedals on the test bike.

Yes. We have a secure 24/7 key-drop locker system on the north side of the building. Simply lock your bike to our monitored drop-off rack, fill out the envelope tag, and drop your key into the slot.

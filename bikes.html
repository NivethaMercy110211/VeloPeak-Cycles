Frame Engineering

Carbon Matrix vs 6061 Double-Butted Alloy

Both frame materials are optimized for performance, durability, and ride feel.

Toray High-Modulus Carbon Matrix

Ultralight & Vibration Dampening

Engineered with directional fiber layups that offer high torsional stiffness at the bottom bracket for sprint power, while naturally absorbing road micro-vibrations for all-day comfort.

  • Average frame weight: 850g – 1,050g
  • Seamless aero-sculpted tube transitions
  • Featured on Apex R1 and AeroStorm Pro builds

6061 Double-Butted Hydroformed Alloy

Maximum Durability & Value

Custom hydroformed aluminum tubes with variable wall thickness — thicker at high-stress weld junctions and thinner in the center to save weight without sacrificing impact toughness.

  • Smooth-weld aesthetic with internal cable ports
  • High dent resistance for trails and commuter racks
  • Featured on MetroFlow, VoltRide and Junior builds

Head Protection

How MIPS Technology Protects Your Brain

Traditional helmets protect against direct radial impacts. MIPS (Multi-directional Impact Protection System) incorporates a low-friction internal layer that allows 10–15mm of relative movement during angled impacts, substantially reducing rotational forces transferred to the brain.

Rotational Force Dissipation:
Reduces strain on brain tissue during real-world oblique crashes.
Optimized Airflow Channels:
MIPS Evolve aligns seamlessly with helmet vents without impeding cooling.
Illumination Science

Choosing the Right Light for Your Route

Matching lumen output and beam pattern to city streets, unlit country roads, or pitch-black trails.

City & Street Lit Roads

200 – 450 Lumens

Purpose: To be seen by drivers. Flashing daytime mode with side cutouts for 180-degree intersection visibility.

Unlit Suburbs & Commuting

600 – 1000 Lumens

Purpose: To see potholes and obstacles at 25km/h. Anti-glare shaped beam that lights 40 meters ahead without blinding oncoming cyclists.

Night Trails & Singletrack

1200 – 2000+ Lumens

Purpose: Broad floodlight for fast downhill navigation. Wide dispersion pattern illuminating corner apexes, rocks and low-hanging branches.
